AI and the Future of U.S. Wealth Management

Industry Portfolio Management and Investment Advice, NAICS 523940 Course RESECON 490AI, UMass Amherst Status In progress, final report December 2026

[PLACEHOLDER] 150–250 word project summary. A study of the U.S. wealth-management industry: how big it is, who the key players are, whether it is growing, and how generative AI is changing advisors' work, fees and entry-level roles. It ends with my own plan for getting into the industry.

What the report covers

  1. The industry: definition, scope and NAICS code

  3. Stage: emerging, growth, mature or declining, with evidence
  4. Data: government, industry and company sources, with charts I made
  5. GenAI impact: roles, productivity, margins, risk, and who gains or loses
